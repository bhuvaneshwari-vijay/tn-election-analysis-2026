# TN 2026 Election Analysis — AtliQ Media

**CodeBasics Resume Project Challenge**

## Project Overview
Data analysis of the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly Election results for a fictional news network, AtliQ Media, who wanted a fact-based 1-hour TV show grounded only in ECI data.

## Research Questions Analysed
- **Q2:** Which seats changed hands between 2021 and 2026?
- **Q3:** Where did TVK's votes actually come from?
- **Q6:** How dominant were the winners in each constituency?

## Key Findings
- 163 of 234 seats (70%) changed hands
- TVK alone flipped 107 seats from DMK, AIADMK and INC
- DMK dropped from 38% → 24.3% state-wide · AIADMK from 33.5% → 21.3%
- TVK led in all 6 regions · highest in Chennai Metro at 46.9%
- Average win percentage in 2026 was just 39% · 61 seats won with under 35% of votes
